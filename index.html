About Althaya

Serene luxury, one session at a time.

ALTHAYA is a therapeutic massage and wellbeing brand in Perth, founded by Laura, a Senior Remedial Massage Therapist with more than 15 years of experience.

It combines professional knowledge, personalised attention and a conscious presence during every session. Treatments are approached as complete experiences of care, adapted to the needs, preferences and comfort of each person.

The essence of ALTHAYA is serene luxury: a warm setting, a close and professional manner, and attention to the smallest details. The goal is to offer a pause in the routine — to rest, release tension and reconnect with the body.

“Through experienced hands and thoughtful touch, each massage becomes a personal ritual of care — a moment to rest, reconnect and feel looked after.”

A note on bookings

ALTHAYA is a space with a gentle focus on women's care. Men are welcome by recommendation of an existing client — please reach out directly to arrange your session.

Deep Relaxation Massage

The Art of Stillness

There are times when the body asks for gentleness — a softer touch, a slower pace, a moment to be cared for.

The Art of Stillness is a deeply comforting massage for times of emotional stress, or simply when you need rest and tenderness. Gentle, flowing movements and attentive care invite you to calm the nervous system, let go of the demands of the day and receive.

Every gesture is guided by sensitivity to your comfort, giving you space to rest without expectation. The experience closes with a guided meditation, with aromas and sound elements adapted to your preferences and comfort.

Intuitive Bodywork

Signature Experience

The Ritual

Every session is a small ceremony of care

The welcome includes a brief pause, three conscious breaths and a choice of aroma, if you wish.

The closing may include breathwork and Tibetan singing bowl, depending on the experience and your personal preferences. Guided meditation is part of The Art of Stillness.

Rituals are offered as optional wellbeing elements, with no medical claims attached.

Frequently asked questions

Which treatment should I book?

Each experience is described above — if you're still unsure, reach out to Laura directly and she'll help you choose.

Can I choose the pressure of the massage?

Yes. It's adapted to your needs, preferences and comfort throughout the session.

How do I book?

Through the BOOK NOW button, which takes you to the online booking system.

Do you offer massage during pregnancy?

Yes, through Sacred Bloom. We collect some information beforehand to assess and adapt your session.

Do you offer mobile / home massage?

Not at this time.

What does my booked time include?

It includes your initial consultation, time to get settled for treatment, the treatment itself, and time to dress afterwards.
