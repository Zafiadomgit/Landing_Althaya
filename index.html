About Althaya

Serene luxury, one session at a time.

ALTHAYA is a therapeutic massage and wellbeing brand in Perth, founded by Laura Montes Soria, a Remedial Massage Therapist with more than 15 years of experience.

It combines professional knowledge, personalised attention and a conscious presence during every session. Treatments are approached as complete experiences of care, adapted to the needs, preferences and comfort of each person.

The essence of ALTHAYA is serene luxury: a warm setting, a close and professional manner, and attention to the smallest details. The goal is to offer a pause in the routine — to rest, release tension and reconnect with the body.

“With my hands and my experience, every massage becomes more than a physical treatment: a ritual of care and wellbeing.”

A note on bookings

ALTHAYA is a space with a gentle focus on women's care. Men are welcome by recommendation of an existing client — please reach out directly to arrange your session.

Treatments

Four experiences, one philosophy of care

Every name is paired with a clear description, so you can choose the experience that meets you where you are.

Sensorial Relaxation

Return to Self

A sensorial relaxation massage with long, flowing movements, warmed oil and an unhurried pace. An experience to disconnect from the demands of the day and enjoy a space of rest. Closes with a guided meditation; aromas and sound elements are adapted to your preference and comfort.

Expert Remedial Massage

Deep Restore

A personalised therapeutic massage that begins with an assessment of your needs and goals. Combines specific techniques and tailored pressure to work areas of tension and support greater ease of movement. The most muscle-focused experience, including goals related to activity and sport. Intensity is agreed with you — it doesn't always mean firm pressure.

Pregnancy Massage

Sacred Bloom

An experience dedicated to caring for women during pregnancy. Offers a space to rest and receive personalised attention, adapting positioning, support and treatment to your comfort and circumstances. The focus is on caring for you through this stage of change, with listening and professionalism.

“Supporting your changing body, honouring the woman you are becoming.”

Intuitive Bodywork

Signature Experience

ALTHAYA's personalised signature experience. Combines therapeutic technique, slow work and moments of relaxation, adjusting to your preferences and your response during the session. Intuition expressed as attention and sensitivity in hands-on work, always paired with communication and professional experience. No rigid sequence.

The Ritual

Every session is a small ceremony of care

The welcome includes a brief pause, three conscious breaths and a choice of aroma, if you wish.

The closing may include breathwork and Tibetan singing bowl, depending on the experience and your personal preferences. Guided meditation is part of Return to Self.

Rituals are offered as optional wellbeing elements, with no medical claims attached.

Frequently asked questions

Which treatment should I book?

Each experience is described above — if you're still unsure, reach out to Laura directly and she'll help you choose.

Can I choose the pressure of the massage?

Yes. It's adapted to your needs, preferences and comfort throughout the session.

How do I book?

Through the BOOK NOW button, which takes you to the online booking system.

Do you offer massage during pregnancy?

Yes, through Sacred Bloom. We collect some information beforehand to assess and adapt your session.

Do you offer mobile / home massage?

Not at this time.

What does my booked time include?

It includes your initial consultation, time to get settled for treatment, the treatment itself, and time to dress afterwards.
