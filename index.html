About Althaya

Serene luxury, one session at a time.

ALTHAYA is a therapeutic massage and wellbeing brand in Perth, founded by Laura, a Senior Remedial Massage Therapist with more than 15 years of experience.

It combines professional knowledge, personalised attention and a conscious presence during every session. Treatments are approached as complete experiences of care, adapted to the needs, preferences and comfort of each person.

The essence of ALTHAYA is serene luxury: a warm setting, a close and professional manner, and attention to the smallest details. The goal is to offer a pause in the routine — to rest, release tension and reconnect with the body.

“Through experienced hands and thoughtful touch, each massage becomes a personal ritual of care — a moment to rest, reconnect and feel looked after.”

Meet Laura

Founder & Senior Remedial Massage Therapist

Massage therapy has been part of my life since I was 18. What began as a fascination with the body gradually became the work I knew I wanted to dedicate myself to - and, more than 15 years later, it still feels deeply meaningful to me.

My approach has been shaped by far more than massage alone. I have a background in osteopathic studies in Spain, which gave me a deeper understanding of structure, movement, compensation and the way different areas of the body influence one another.

Over the years, I have also worked alongside physiotherapists, chiropractors and naturopaths, as well as in luxury spa environments in Barcelona and Australia. Moving between clinical and wellness settings taught me something important: effective bodywork does not have to choose between expertise and care. It can hold both.

For me, each treatment is a form of meditation. When I work, I am fully present - observing, listening with my hands and continuously adapting to the person in front of me.

No two sessions are the same because no two people arrive needing the same thing.

Sometimes the body needs precision, strength and focused therapeutic work. Sometimes it needs softness, stillness and permission to let go. Often, it needs both.

Over the years, I have had the privilege of being part of important turning points in many people's wellbeing - helping someone move with less pain, reconnect with their body, release long-held tension, or simply experience what it feels like to stop and be genuinely cared for.

There is space for expression in my treatment room. Space to talk, to be quiet, to breathe, to release, to laugh, to feel, or simply to receive.

My work brings together experience, therapeutic knowledge, intuition, strength and care.

That philosophy became ALTHAYA: a space where expertise meets presence, and where every treatment is approached with intention, attention and genuine care.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which treatment should I book?

Each experience is described above — if you're still unsure, reach out to Laura directly and she'll help you choose.

Can I choose the pressure of the massage?

Yes. It's adapted to your needs, preferences and comfort throughout the session.

How do I book?

Through the BOOK NOW button, which takes you to the online booking system.

Do you offer massage during pregnancy?

Yes, through Bloom. We collect some information beforehand to assess and adapt your session.

Do you offer mobile / home massage?

Not at this time.

What does my booked time include?

It includes your initial consultation, time to get settled for treatment, the treatment itself, and time to dress afterwards.

Can I use my private health insurance?

Yes — via HICAPS. You're able to use your private health "Extras" cover to claim for remedial massage.

Do you treat male clients?

ALTHAYA has a particular focus on women's wellbeing. Male clients are currently welcomed by referral from an existing client.
